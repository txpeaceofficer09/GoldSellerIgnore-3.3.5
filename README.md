## Gold Seller Ignore ##

This AddOn does not actually place anyone on your ignore list.  Instead it filters messages and silently drops them before you ever see a gold seller message.  It does this by looking for keywords like "gold", "cheap", "safe", etc that are common in the gold seller spam messages.  If enough keywords are found in the message then the message is just silently dropped.

If everyone places them on ignore, it fills up your ignore list and then they are also notified they are being ignored.  What do they do when all their messages are getting ignored? Create a new character/account and start the bot again sending out their messages.  If their messages are just dropped then, as far as they know, you are reading their messages.
